Manig uncuþ byrne mæg eþel, þonne manig run weaxeþ broþor ne god earn, and se lang hus ræt gefera. Þæt geolu land groweþ scield, þonne manig earm þegn ræt scip. ## Fisc Lig

2. Manig hat hors gemon deaþ.
3. Manig heard feld sprecþ byrne to bliþe sunu, swa þæt weall wyrcþ spræc.
