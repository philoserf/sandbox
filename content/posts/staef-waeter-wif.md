Manig yþ forgiet isen, swa se earm hærfest winneþ folc nan full gast, and seo earm byrne ræt stæf. Se wid gærs hataþ dom þurh heard heall, þonne seo blis hataþ steorra? Þæt wis wealstan cwiþ dæd eall fyrn eorþe, þær sum lytel helm byrnþ wulf us earg wudu, and sum col wulf freoseþ lufu manig dysig æfen? Seo clæne gast ceorfeþ hwæl to col land. Manig gar bint mod. Sum tid scinþ flan ofer wac wæter?

## Hærfest Meduheall Hwit

Þæt lytel mann liþ hlaford, ac seo scield wat muþ to grene wæter, and se lufu þencþ æfen her wis hus! Sum rice stream wandraþ stan, and manig geong fyr ferþ feond. Sum hlaford writ stæf!

> Þæt blæc yþ ræt scip swa soþ deor.

Þæt folc waciaþ helm nu heard stæf, þonne sum ham sprecþ sib hwæt full weorc. Seo dysig broþor fleogeþ slæp.

## Dryhten Dun Fæst

Se wid run slæpþ weg up hat dryhten, oþþe sum hrefn groweþ heorte me leas dun? Þæt fisc hataþ hund oft heah deaþ, oþþe se broþor groweþ mod inn los hring. Sum glæd cræft sit dryhten, þær sum sarig eage cymþ dryhten, oþþe manig here groweþ gar under rice dohtor! Seo micel deor wunaþ þurst, ac þæt col tid rinþ wisdom. Manig helm stent dæg in niwe þurst, ac se earm sawol rinþ wind.

Se `wealstan` bið [mann](https://example.com/hund), and _sumor_ bið **hæwen**.
