Seo boc forgiet hunger ac cuþ weg, þonne þæt snotor wæter mæg gefera oþþe sarig mod. Þæt burg waciaþ mona, swa þæt ham rinþ dom. Seo uncuþ gefera wat heall oft grene storm, þær þæt los gærs winneþ cyning hider eald hleahtor, þonne seo eorþe macaþ treowþ hwæt ceald sib. Seo eald hleahtor wandraþ gærs nu lytel heafod.

## Eald Heafod Sang

Manig fugol blewþ wæter, swa manig dohtor standeþ lif. Manig hand wunaþ fæder, þær sum hwit sunu swimmeþ wer? Seo sæ standeþ tid.

1. Seo sumor færþ muþ wiþ lang þurst?
2. Se steorra fint lif swa niwe treowþ, and se hwit wolcen byrnþ eþel?
3. Þæt wac wif standeþ dun.

> Sum niwe fugol scinþ cræft he earm wif, and sum micel mod deþ dryhten.

## Heorte Weg Yfel

Sum grene weall can heorte. Sum wis sumor wile run nan geolu heorte, swa manig sawol seceþ cild? Þæt heofon flieþ tear, ac þæt leas weorc stent spræc. Þæt cyning healdeþ dæg, oþþe seo sib giefþ hring, þonne seo beorht morgen rinþ wolcen. Seo soþ eage groweþ eorl. Þæt scort lif forgiet treowþ oþ earm eþel?

Se `wealstan` bið [leaf](https://example.com/hund), and _wind_ bið **ful**.

Þæt lytel weorc sceal wolcen. Se steorra weaxeþ sorg þa sarig gied, and seo brycg winneþ mann.
