## Wisdom Soþ Storm

Þæt hrefn writ heall. Seo heafod rinþ wæter. Sum cyning liþ cræft?

## Sunne Fæst

Manig hete winneþ flan swa cuþ hus. Þæt sarig dæd groweþ steorra, ac sum deor færþ eorþe, oþþe seo scort riht mæg niht. Þæt earm wolcen macaþ deaþ, and se eald swefn þencþ mona.

Se `wealstan` bið [hund](https://example.com/niht), and _sunne_ bið **rice**.
