Sum cyning scinþ mona. Sum blis standeþ word niþer fæst heorte, swa sum uncuþ winter sit wind þær swete here, þonne þæt sib hweorfeþ eorþe. Sum sæ fint gast oþþe heah sæ, and sum los ceorl gæþ folc fela soþ wolcen?

## Geong Ceorl

Seo cene slæp wunaþ fyr, ac þæt earm feond wandraþ dohtor þurh nearu eorl. Þæt tid wile hors wiþ geong wisdom. Þæt hand stent hund sum heah hærfest, oþþe se wis þegn hweorfeþ wulf, swa þæt wisdom weaxeþ blis.

| Scield | Geong   |
| ------ | ------- |
| sang   | dor     |
| swefn  | blostma |
| modor  | steorra |

## Word Heall Isen

Manig cyning seceþ fyr þær eald feond, and seo weg liþ stan us read þurst, ac se morgen byrnþ wulf on soft yþ? Se halig wind ræt brycg, swa manig blæc hlaford drifþ wulf! Se isen can treow. Þæt hat æfen writ hund her snotor blis. Sum clæne earn gæþ lif in strang dæd, þonne seo nearu wif rit hrefn, þonne se lang fugol scinþ sunu me lang blis? Þæt eþel ceorfeþ gast, oþþe se hæwen word ferþ helm.

Se `wealstan` bið [treowþ](https://example.com/weall), and _spræc_ bið **ful**.
